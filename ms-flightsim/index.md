# MS_FlightSim

The aviation career for FiveM: players train for a licence, rank up, buy aircraft and fly 16 paid missions, while a tower, AI traffic and a ground crew bring the airport to life.

## What's in the box

- **Flight school** — theory exam and practical test for planes and helicopters, then six specialist ratings.
- **Career** — 10 pilot ranks earned by flying, a logbook with badges, and a safety record with strikes.
- **Missions** — cargo, passengers, sightseeing, survey, crop dusting, banner towing, sky writing, offshore supply, airliner flights, smuggling, heavy lift, medevac, wildfire suppression, supply drops, VIP extraction, aerial firefighting, and airshows.
- **Aircraft ownership** — dealer with buy and rent, hangar, insurance, fuel, repair, maintenance and paint.
- **Air traffic control** — player controllers, a three-screen tower console, flight plans, runway locks and pre-recorded ATC voice.
- **The airport** — AI aircraft parked at stands, a ground-services job anyone can do, and a fleet of ground vehicles.
- **Companies** — a pilot company with a shared fund, and player-run sub-companies that dispatch real AI flights carrying their logo.
- **Cockpit** — movable PFD/HUD, autopilot with heading and altitude selectors, autoland, auto-hover, TCAS, TAWS, wind and malfunctions.
- **Admin panel** (`/adminps`) — players, zones, airports, screens, aircraft, jobs and the economy, all in game.
